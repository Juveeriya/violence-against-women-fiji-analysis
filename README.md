# Violence Against Women in Fiji — Trend Analysis

Analysis of trends in reported cases of violence against women in Fiji (2015–2022), with a comparative look at 2022 vs. January–August 2023 data. Produced during a research placement with **FemLINK Pacific**, a Fiji-based organisation working on gender justice and media empowerment in the Pacific region, as part of a Think Pacific fieldwork internship.

## Overview

The project examines reported case data across six categories of violence against women — domestic violence, rape, attempted rape, other sexual offences, adult survivor cases, and sexual harassment — to surface trends, year-on-year shifts, and population-level prevalence, and to connect the findings to FemLINK Pacific's advocacy strategy.

**Key research questions:**
- What are the key differences and similarities in reported cases of violence against women in Fiji between 2022 and the first three quarters of 2023?
- What do these trends suggest about the evolving nature of violence against women in Fiji, and their implications for ongoing challenges?
- Are there notable patterns that warrant further investigation or targeted interventions?
- What strategies and collaborative actions are essential to realizing FemLINK's vision of gender justice and equality in the Pacific?

## Data Sources

- [Fiji Women's Crisis Centre (FWCC) statistics](https://www.fijiwomen.com/publications/statistics/fiji-womens-crisis-centre-statistics/)
- Fiji National Action Plan to Prevent Violence Against All Women and Girls 2023–2028
- FWCC National Survey Summary
- [World Bank — Fiji female population data](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL.FE.IN?end=2022&locations=FJ&start=2015)

Data spans 2015–2022 for trend analysis, plus a 2022 vs. Jan–Aug 2023 comparison. Child-specific categories (child rape, child sexual abuse, child physical/emotional/verbal abuse) were excluded as out of scope.

## Key Findings

- **Domestic violence** is the most prevalent category, totaling 4,172 reported cases from 2015–2022, though it declined 19.72% comparing 2022 to Jan–Aug 2023.
- **Rape** increased 14.29% and **other sexual offences** increased 33.33% over the same comparison period.
- **Sexual harassment** dropped sharply, down 84.62%, while **attempted rape** stayed effectively flat (0.00% change).
- Relative to Fiji's female population, roughly **1 in 47 women** report sexual harassment, **1 in 70** rape, and **1 in 100** domestic violence — the three most common forms.
